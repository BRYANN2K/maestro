# design-system — sources

Editorial synthesis based on research reviewed on September 7, 2026. The phase procedures are our adaptation to the v1 contract, not certification by the cited organizations. Verify the version and applicability of evolving standards or documentation before deriving a current requirement.

- [Contribution criteria](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/community/contribution-criteria/) — GOV.UK Design System; useful/unique proposals, usable/consistent/versatile implementations, and testing evidence.
- [Develop a component or pattern](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/community/develop-a-component-or-pattern/) — GOV.UK Design System; shared plans, research, community, accessibility, and review before publication.
- [Extending and modifying components in production](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/get-started/extending-and-modifying-components/) — GOV.UK Design System; overrides, namespaces, forks, debt, and accessibility checks.
- [Get started](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/get-started/) — GOV.UK Design System; component usage and caution around untested ideas.
- [Design tokens](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/) — U.S. Web Design System; discrete values supporting consistency and communication.
- [Accessibility](https://designsystem.digital.gov/documentation/accessibility/) — U.S. Web Design System; automated/manual tests, assistive technology, browsers, feedback, and page-level checks.

All pages were consulted on 2026-09-07. Steps and `AC-DS-*` candidates are a synthesis adapted to Stip.
