# frontend-engineering — sources

Editorial synthesis based on research reviewed on September 7, 2026. The phase procedures are our adaptation to the v1 contract, not certification by the cited organizations. Verify the version and applicability of evolving standards or documentation before deriving a current requirement.

- [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/) — W3C; principles, testable criteria, levels, and automated plus human evaluation.
- [ARIA Authoring Practices Guide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/) — W3C; widget patterns, roles, states, accessible names, and keyboard behavior.
- [Core Web Vitals](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals) — Google Search Central; LCP/INP/CLS, thresholds, and real-user measurement.
- [Progressive enhancement](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Glossary/Progressive_Enhancement) — MDN Web Docs; usable baseline followed by capability-based enhancement.

Integration statuses, steps, and criteria are a synthesis for Stip; sources describe contextual practices or standards rather than prescribing this contract.
