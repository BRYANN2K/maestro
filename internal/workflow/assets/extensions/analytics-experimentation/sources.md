# analytics-experimentation — sources

Editorial synthesis based on research reviewed on September 7, 2026. The phase procedures are our adaptation to the v1 contract, not certification by the cited organizations. Verify the version and applicability of evolving standards or documentation before deriving a current requirement.

- [Measuring the User Experience on a Large Scale](https://research.google/pubs/measuring-the-user-experience-on-a-large-scale-user-centered-metrics-for-web-applications/) — Google Research; HEART and goals/signals/metrics.
- [Experimentation Platform (ExP)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/experimentation-platform-exp/) — Microsoft Research; hypothesis validation, actual impact, and safe iteration.
- [Stats Engine deep dive](https://certification.optimizely.com/docs/concepts/experimentation/stats-engine-deep-dive/) — Optimizely; sequential testing, always-valid p-values, FDR, MDE, and CUPED examples.
- [Diagnosing Sample Ratio Mismatch in A/B Testing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/diagnosing-sample-ratio-mismatch-in-a-b-testing/) — Microsoft Research/ExP; SRM, chi-squared testing, staged diagnosis, and Microsoft's documented conservative threshold.
- [Patterns of Trustworthy Experimentation: During-Experiment Stage](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/patterns-of-trustworthy-experimentation-during-experiment-stage/) — Microsoft Research/ExP; repeated measurements, peeking, multiple testing, temporal segmentation, and predetermined duration.

All pages were consulted on 2026-09-07. Optimizely's method is explicitly a vendor implementation; the criteria are our local synthesis.
