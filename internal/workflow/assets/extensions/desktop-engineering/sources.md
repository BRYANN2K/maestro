- [Capabilities](https://v2.tauri.app/security/capabilities/) — Tauri; explicit window/webview permissions and capability-model limits.
- [Windows apps](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/) — Microsoft Learn; Windows App SDK/WinUI, design, development, packaging, and deployment.
- [Designing inclusive software](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/accessibility/designing-inclusive-software) — Microsoft Learn; accessibility throughout design and quality work.
- [AppKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appkit) — Apple Developer Documentation; native macOS UI, events, accessibility, and AppKit/SwiftUI integration.
- [Distributing software on macOS](https://developer.apple.com/macos/distribution/) — Apple Developer; Mac App Store or Developer ID distribution, Gatekeeper, signing, and notarization.

All pages were consulted on 2026-09-07. The extension contract and criteria are a local synthesis selected according to the capabilities actually involved.
