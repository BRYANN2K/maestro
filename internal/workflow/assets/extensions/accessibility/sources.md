# accessibility — sources

Editorial synthesis based on research reviewed on September 7, 2026. The phase procedures are our adaptation to the v1 contract, not certification by the cited organizations. Verify the version and applicability of evolving standards or documentation before deriving a current requirement.

- [WCAG 2 Overview](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) — W3C WAI; POUR principles, testable criteria, conformance levels, and WCAG 2.2.
- [Introduction — ARIA Authoring Practices Guide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/about/introduction/) — W3C WAI; informative APG role, patterns, and limits relative to normative standards.
- [Developing a Keyboard Interface](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/practices/keyboard-interface/) — W3C WAI; focus, Tab sequence, and ARIA widget keyboard support.
- [Making your service accessible: an introduction](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/helping-people-to-use-your-service/making-your-service-accessible-an-introduction) — GOV.UK Service Manual; early accessibility, team responsibility, disabled-user research, and discovery/alpha/beta stages.
- [Getting an accessibility audit](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/helping-people-to-use-your-service/getting-an-accessibility-audit) — GOV.UK Service Manual; audit samples, WCAG 2.2 AA, assistive-technology testing, and follow-up.
- [Accessibility](https://designsystem.digital.gov/documentation/accessibility/) — U.S. Web Design System; POUR principles, ongoing adaptation, limits of universal-accessibility promises, and community feedback.

All six pages were consulted on 2026-09-07. W3C standards and cited obligations retain their own scope; steps, `AC-A11Y-*` candidates, and evidence levels are our integration synthesis, not certification.
