## Domain procedure

Clarify audience, intent, and expected action. Connect each promise to a capability, observation, or measurement and its limitations. Build a central message, supporting messages, evidence, and objections. Translate the narrative into concrete surfaces without distorting facts. Test paraphrasing by a representative reader and correct contradictions with the product. Prepare content; publish or contact audiences only when authorized.

Break down this procedure according to the change's risks; steps may overlap or be not-applicable with justification. Start from reusable foundations identified during explore. Verify expected behavior incrementally and correct within the contract; use [check.md](check.md) for domain observations.

If the work reveals a new requirement, return to contract revision and approval; do not move a threshold to make a result pass.
