# storytelling — sources

Editorial synthesis based on research reviewed on September 7, 2026. The phase procedures are our adaptation to the v1 contract, not certification by the cited organizations. Verify the version and applicability of evolving standards or documentation before deriving a current requirement.

- [Keeping the market in mind: What makes product marketing successful at Intercom?](https://www.intercom.com/blog/product-marketing-at-intercom/) — Intercom; market perspective, strategy, positioning, messages, and adoption.
- [For better products, start with a problem statement](https://www.intercom.com/blog/how-to-write-problem-statements/) — Intercom; desired outcomes, reasons, and status-quo pain as problem/solution foundations.
- [Hook trial users from their first use](https://www.intercom.com/blog/trial-users-first-use/) — Intercom; first value, jobs to be done, demonstration, and fewer onboarding steps.
- [Presentation storytelling 101: Your audience is the hero](https://www.duarte.com/blog/presentation-storytelling-audience-is-hero/) — Duarte; audience as hero and presenter as guide.
- [The Duarte Big Idea](https://www.duarte.com/training/duarte-big-idea/) — Duarte; central ideas before media and content selection.
- [New public workshop](https://www.storytellingwithdata.com/new-public-workshop) — Storytelling with Data; audience/message, storyboards, visual choices, and noise reduction.

All six pages were consulted on 2026-09-07. Intercom, Duarte, and Storytelling with Data practices are contextual references; steps, `AC-ST-*` candidates, and evidence rules are a core-adapted synthesis, not a universal standard.
