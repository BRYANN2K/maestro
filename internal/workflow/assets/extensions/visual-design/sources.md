# visual-design — sources

Editorial synthesis based on research reviewed on September 7, 2026. The phase procedures are our adaptation to the v1 contract, not certification by the cited organizations. Verify the version and applicability of evolving standards or documentation before deriving a current requirement.

- [Colour](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/colour/) — GOV.UK Design System; functional palette, contrast, and color meaning.
- [Type scale](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/type-scale/) — GOV.UK Design System; tested scale, responsiveness, and readability.
- [Layout](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/layout/) — GOV.UK Design System; small-first layouts, grids, and line length.
- [Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography) — Apple Human Interface Guidelines; readability, hierarchy, and Dynamic Type.
- [Color](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color) — Apple Human Interface Guidelines; consistency, themes, and alternatives to color alone.
- [Design principles](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles) — Apple Human Interface Guidelines; principles as decision tools rather than a single recipe.

All pages were consulted on 2026-09-07. Integration steps and `AC-VD-*` candidates are a synthesis adapted to Stip.
