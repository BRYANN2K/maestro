# ux-design — sources

Editorial synthesis based on research reviewed on September 7, 2026. The phase procedures are our adaptation to the v1 contract, not certification by the cited organizations. Verify the version and applicability of evolving standards or documentation before deriving a current requirement.

- [Framework for Innovation](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/) — Design Council; Double Diamond, divergence/convergence, and iterative loops.
- [Making prototypes](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/making-prototypes) — GOV.UK Service Manual; prototyping before commitment, fidelity, and prototype-code limits.
- [How the alpha phase works](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/how-the-alpha-phase-works) — GOV.UK Service Manual; risky assumptions, targeted prototypes, inclusion, and progression decisions.
- [User research in alpha](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/user-research-in-alpha) — GOV.UK Service Manual; solution research, interactive prototypes, and needs coverage.
- [What Is User Experience?](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/what-is-user-experience/) — Nielsen Norman Group; holistic experience beyond UI; consulted through search results, with limited direct access.

All pages were consulted on 2026-09-07. The criteria, `AC-UX-*` candidates, and selection rules are an integration synthesis for Stip.
