# database-engineering — sources

Editorial synthesis based on research reviewed on September 7, 2026. The phase procedures are our adaptation to the v1 contract, not certification by the cited organizations. Verify the version and applicability of evolving standards or documentation before deriving a current requirement.

- [Concurrency Control](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/mvcc.html) — PostgreSQL 17, the reviewed edition for MVCC, visibility, isolation, concurrency, and deadlocks.
- [Using EXPLAIN](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/using-explain.html) — PostgreSQL 17, the reviewed edition for estimated plans/costs and executed/measured `EXPLAIN ANALYZE`.
- [Atomic Commit In SQLite](https://www.sqlite.org/atomiccommit.html) — SQLite; atomic commits, locks, failures, and rollback.
- [Database Security Cheat Sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Database_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html) — OWASP; isolation, TLS, authentication, least privilege, and API access.

All pages were consulted on 2026-09-07. Criteria, statuses, and depth levels are a synthesis for the Stip contract.
